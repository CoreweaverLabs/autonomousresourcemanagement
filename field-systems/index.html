Field index / working frame

Where resource decisions meet the field.

Field Systems is a bounded index for teams examining how resource decisions, constraints, escalation, and retained records appear in different operating contexts. It is a framing tool, not a deployed control system.

Surface

Reference index for applied questions.

Authority

Inquiry and method context only.

Method anchor

ARM primitives and explicit human escalation.

01Decision route

Start with what must be inspectable.

This five-part route adapts the ARM reference to a field context. It helps structure a question before assuming a system, product, integration, or outcome.

01Observe

Name the resource, its operating context, and the signals available for review.

02Compare

Make the constraint, threshold, or trade-off explicit instead of treating it as implicit.

03Constrain

Define the authority and limit that governs what a system may recommend or do.

04Escalate

Specify where uncertainty, conflict, or exception returns to accountable human review.

05Retain

Keep an inspectable record of the decision, its basis, and its permitted next step.

02Field index

A field index, not a claim of operation.

Each context below is a route for inquiry. It does not establish that ARM controls equipment, delivers an outcome, or is endorsed by an external organization.

F.01

Energy-system decisions

Consider how resource constraints, dispatch assumptions, escalation paths, and retained decision records could be framed in energy-system contexts.

BoundaryLinks to an applied method discussion; it does not claim operation or optimization of an energy system.
F.02

Industrial automation contexts

Examine the decision boundary between observation, interpretation, permitted action, and operator review in industrial settings.

BoundaryDoes not assert deployment within an industrial control system or any safety, reliability, or performance effect.
F.03

Process and material routes

Map the questions that surface around process state, material movement, monitoring, inspection, and accountable handoffs.

BoundaryNames application topics; it does not replace engineering review, safety controls, or operating procedures.
F.04

Evidence and governance

Keep the claim, source, media record, review owner, and next decision visible as a system moves from framework to application.

BoundaryA cited framework informs context; it is not certification, endorsement, or compliance assurance.

03Application boundary

Use a field to sharpen a question, not enlarge a promise.

Application begins with a bounded decision.

Use this index to structure a discussion of decision routes, source context, and escalation boundaries before representing a tool, implementation, or result.

What this index supports: a structured discussion of decision routes, sources, and escalation boundaries. What it does not support: a claim that a particular field implementation is safe, compliant, autonomous, certified, or already in operation.
